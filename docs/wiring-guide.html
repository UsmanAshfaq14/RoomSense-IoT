RoomSense Wiring Guide

Two breadboards on one Raspberry Pi 3. Breadboard 1 holds the sensors (BME680, VEML7700, RDM6300 RFID). Breadboard 2 holds the outputs and the PIR (three LEDs, PIR, FS90R) and takes its 5 V and GND from breadboard 1. The guide opens at step 5, where breadboard 2 starts.
